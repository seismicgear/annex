# Using, extending, and selling Annex

The [Annex Community License 2.1](../LICENSE.md) permits commercial use and
modification while reserving resale and paid hosting for a separate agreement.
The license text controls; this page is a practical guide to the intended terms.

| Activity | Community license |
| --- | --- |
| Run Annex for yourself, a school, nonprofit, or your own business | Allowed |
| Modify it, build an integration, or keep an internal fork | Allowed |
| Run a community server with free access | Allowed |
| Share copies or a clearly identified fork for free | Allowed; retain the license and notices |
| Charge for consulting, installation, or support on a customer's own deployment | Allowed; do not sell a license or hosted service as part of that charge |
| Sell Annex, a rebranded fork, or paid access to hosted Annex | Separate written reseller agreement required |
| Use KI's unrelated projects, confidential IP, or trademarks as your own | Not granted |

Kuykendall Industries LLC owns Annex's KI-owned code and Contributions assigned
under the [Contributor Assignment Agreement](../CONTRIBUTOR_ASSIGNMENT.md).
Receiving source code gives recipients the permissions in the community
license; it does not transfer KI's ownership. Private modifications and
independently developed additions that are not submitted to KI remain outside
the contribution assignment.

Because paid redistribution and hosted resale are restricted, these are
**source-available terms, not OSI open source**. The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd)
requires free redistribution and use in every field of endeavor. An OSI license
could allow business use and retain copyright, but could not impose this
reseller restriction. “Free to use” and “source available” accurately describe
Annex's community offering.

The unchanged v0.1.0 installers were built with the previous noncommercial
license text. Section 10 of the current license additionally offers KI-owned
v0.1.0 material under these broader terms. Keep that grant with any redistribution;
do not replace release binaries or claim their original bundled text changed.
Third-party notices and licenses still apply; see
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Contributing to KI

Original work submitted for inclusion in Annex must be assigned to Kuykendall
Industries LLC. Each contributing rightsholder or authorized representative
signs the [Contributor Assignment Agreement](../CONTRIBUTOR_ASSIGNMENT.md)
before merge, using the declaration in the PR template. Existing KI ownership
or a written assignment covering the work can be identified instead.

KI may reuse, modify, combine, sell, host, sublicense, and relicense assigned
contributions in any current or future project or product, including
proprietary and reseller offerings, without further contributor approval or
compensation. Those rights are independent of the community license offered
to recipients.

Contributors must disclose third-party material and its terms. Material that
would restrict KI's reuse or impose licensing obligations on other KI code
requires KI's express written approval before acceptance. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full process.

For paid hosting or resale by recipients, contact Kuykendall Industries through
<https://kuykendall.industries>.
