# Annex product and privacy notice

Version 2026-11-07-portfolio-1 · Effective November 7, 2026

## Your software and your server

Kuykendall Industries, LLC (KI) provides Annex software. The person or organization operating the server you join controls that community and its records. Running a server on your own computer makes you its operator. Review the server's rules and privacy information before joining or sharing information.

The [Annex Community License](https://github.com/seismicgear/annex/blob/main/LICENSE.md) governs the software. Existing licenses, purchased rights and separate written agreements remain in effect. This notice does not change those grants or create a subscription, reseller agreement or support entitlement. Independent components, speech tools and models have their own licenses.

## Identity and local storage

Annex keeps identity keys, device and encryption material, saved servers, settings, session information and recovery data on your device or in browser storage. You may export an identity, link another device, or choose supported Array wallet backup and recovery. Those actions create additional copies. Protect backup files and recovery information; possession can allow access to your identity. An email address or phone number is not required to create an Annex identity.

## Information shared with a server and other people

Your selected server processes identity proofs and pseudonyms, membership and permission records, profile information you provide, messages, files, search requests, moderation and security events, and connection information. Authorized members, administrators, agents and configured federation peers may receive information according to the channel and server settings. Pseudonyms do not make connection addresses, timing or membership anonymous.

Ordinary channels let the server process message content, even when it is encrypted at rest. End-to-end encryption is selected per channel; authorized recipients can still read, save and share what they receive. Text and attachment encryption does not establish end-to-end encryption for calls, camera, screen sharing or metadata.

## Calls, agents and connected services

Microphone, camera and screen sharing send the selected media to the call participants and the configured media service. A server's speech features may process audio, transcripts and generated speech with its selected tools or providers. An admitted agent may receive channel content and send it to the external runtime or model provider its operator configures. Review the server's setup before using these features. Permission to connect an agent, account or server is separate from permission to publish information or perform other actions.

Configured federation and knowledge sharing can send permitted records to other servers or agents. Share only information you are entitled to disclose. KI does not acquire permission to train its models on private community content merely because you use Annex or contact support.

## KI website, invitations and support

KI's website and invitation services process the requests and connection information needed to deliver pages, downloads, invitations and peer introductions. A supported direct connection carries community traffic between peers after introduction; the website is not the community's message archive. The website's [privacy notice](https://monolithannex.com/privacy) explains its hosting, analytics and routing. Download hosts, your server's providers and any connected service have their own handling practices.

Information you choose to send KI for support is handled under [KI's privacy policy](https://kuykendall.industries/privacy). Review diagnostics and attachments before sharing them. Do not include private keys, recovery secrets or invitation credentials in a public report.

## Retention, removal and requests

Device records remain until removed through the relevant app, browser or operating-system controls. Server records follow that operator's retention settings, legal obligations and backup practices. Available message expiration or deletion controls do not erase copies already held by recipients, other servers, exported files or backups. Signing out, disconnecting a server or revoking access is not a request to erase every copy.

For access, correction, export or deletion of community records, contact the server operator. Operators are responsible for providing their own contact route, handling requests within applicable deadlines, and configuring retention and connected services lawfully. For information held by KI, use [Privacy / Data Request](https://kuykendall.industries/contact?category=Privacy%20%2F%20Data%20Request) or call +1 208-500-2410. Identify the service and request without sending identity secrets. KI will verify requests as needed and apply the rights, exceptions and response periods required by applicable law.
