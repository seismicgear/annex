# Kuykendall Industries Contributor Assignment Agreement

Version 1.0 — October 21, 2026

Kuykendall Industries LLC ("KI") accepts original contributions to Annex on the
ownership-assignment terms below. Acceptance of these terms is a condition of
contributing work for inclusion in the repository.

## 1. Contribution and rightsholder

"Contribution" means the original code, documentation, designs, tests, and other
material intentionally submitted for inclusion in Annex and identified in your
signed acceptance. "You" means the rightsholder identified in that acceptance;
a representative must be authorized to bind that rightsholder.

The signed scope identifies the repository and PR or commit(s). It may
expressly include your later original changes intentionally submitted in the
same PR before merge. It does not include unrelated work that you have not
submitted.

## 2. Assignment to KI

You hereby assign to KI all of your right, title, and interest in each
Contribution covered by your signed acceptance, including all copyrights and
other assignable intellectual-property rights, worldwide for the full duration
of those rights. For later Contributions expressly covered by the signed scope,
the assignment takes effect upon submission.

KI owns the assigned Contributions and may use, reproduce, modify, combine,
publish, distribute, sell, host, sublicense, relicense, transfer, and otherwise
commercialize them in any current or future project, product, service, or
reseller offering, under proprietary, source-available, open-source, or other
terms selected by KI, without further permission or compensation to you.
These rights are not limited to Annex or its community distribution license.

No contribution gives its contributor ownership, approval rights, or licensing
control over KI's existing code, other contributions, or other projects.
Attribution and commit authorship do not reserve ownership of assigned work.

## 3. Additional rights

To the extent any rights in a Contribution are not effectively assigned, you
grant KI a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive, transferable,
sublicensable, fully paid-up, royalty-free license to exercise those rights for
all purposes described in section 2.

You also grant KI a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive,
transferable, sublicensable, fully paid-up, royalty-free license under patent

or in combination with the project as submitted, to make, have made, use,

and that combination. This grant follows the covered Contribution when reused
in another KI project.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you waive and agree not to assert
moral rights in the Contribution against KI or parties authorized by KI.

## 4. Authority and material from other sources

You confirm that you own the rights assigned and licensed here or are
authorized by their owner to grant them, including any required employer or
client authorization. You must identify all contributing rightsholders.

Do not submit material you cannot provide on these terms. Identify preexisting
third-party code, dependencies, copied or generated material, and their
applicable licenses or provider terms in the PR. Do not introduce terms that
restrict KI's commercial reuse or relicensing, or impose source-disclosure or
licensing obligations on other KI code or projects, without KI's express
written approval before acceptance.

Approved third-party material remains under its identified license; this
agreement assigns only rights you own or are authorized to assign. Preserve
required third-party notices. KI may reject or require replacement of material
that does not meet its contribution requirements.

## 5. Signed acceptance and records

Before merge, each contributing rightsholder or its authorized representative
must sign this agreement for the covered Contributions. Use the declaration in
[the PR template](.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md), posted from the signer's
own authenticated account, or a separate signed writing identifying this
agreement and the covered work. The signer must expressly intend the typed
legal name to be an electronic signature. A maintainer must not sign for
another rightsholder without authority.

Record the agreement version and repository commit containing it, the PR and
covered commit(s), the rightsholder, the signer's name and authority, and the
date. Retain that signed record with the contribution. Additional rightsholders
must sign for their own material.

Work already owned by KI or covered by an existing written assignment giving
KI these rights does not need a duplicate assignment; identify that basis in
the PR. KI acceptance does not promise payment or a release.

## 6. Scope and existing rights

The [Annex Community License](LICENSE.md) governs permissions granted to
recipients. It does not restrict KI's ownership or commercial rights obtained
under this agreement. No further project-specific contributor approval is
needed to exercise those rights.

This agreement takes effect for each rightsholder through its signed
acceptance. Earlier Contributions may be covered by expressly identifying them
in that acceptance. Existing KI ownership, written assignments, and licenses
already granted remain effective. Any exception to these contribution terms
requires a written agreement executed by KI.
